- **Conversation Explorer**: What users ask AI about your industry/brand
- **Profound Agents** (new Feb 2026): Auto-generate and distribute marketing copy in brand voice
- **Prompt volume tracking**: Real search demand data, not synthetic prompts

- **Profound University**: Courses, certifications, cohort programs (moat-building)

### Market Position

- **G2 rank**: #1 in AEO tools (Winter 2026)

### Strengths
- Category-defining platform with massive data moat (prompt volume + crawler analytics + AI results)

### Weaknesses
- **Starter plan is a joke**: $99/mo for ChatGPT-only monitoring is deliberately crippled

### Endexa Opportunity
Profound owns enterprise. Don't compete there. Their deliberately crippled lower tiers create a massive gap for a tool that gives full multi-engine coverage at $29-49/mo.

## Market Gaps Endexa Can Exploit

### Gap 1: No affordable SEO+GEO combined tool
Every GEO-native tool (Profound, Otterly, Geneo, Geoptie) ignores traditional SEO. Every SEO tool (Semrush, Ahrefs, BrightEdge) bolts GEO on as an expensive add-on. Nobody combines both at an affordable price point. Frase comes closest but is content-only, not technical.

**Endexa position**: The only tool under $50/mo that scores both SEO fundamentals AND GEO readiness in a single audit.

### Gap 2: No tool provides actionable, prioritized recommendations
Every competitor shows you dashboards and scores. None tell you exactly what to fix, in what order, with what priority. Even Frase's recommendations are content-level suggestions, not technical fixes.

**Endexa position**: Priority-ranked recommendations with Jira-ready export. "Fix your robots.txt to allow GPTBot" is more valuable than "Your visibility score is 34%."

### Gap 3: No tool does code-level analysis
Every competitor is crawl-based. None inspect source code, framework configuration, build output, or SSR setup. This matters because AI crawlers don't execute JavaScript -- SSR issues are invisible to crawl-only tools but critical for GEO.

**Endexa position**: Code analysis flow (user runs Endexa-generated prompts on repo, uploads output) is unique in the entire market.

### Gap 4: Steep price cliff between "basic monitoring" and "real value"
Otterly Lite ($29/15 prompts) and Geoptie Starter ($49/15 prompts) are near-useless at their entry tiers. Real value starts at $189+ (Otterly Standard) or $99+ (Geoptie Professional). Semrush's AI Toolkit costs $99/mo for just 25 prompts on 1 domain.

**Endexa position**: Full-featured audit + recommendations at $29-49/mo, not a crippled entry tier.

### Gap 5: No tool targets developer/technical teams
Profound targets enterprise marketing. Otterly targets SMB marketers. Frase targets writers. Geoptie targets beginners. Nobody builds for the developer/agency technical team who needs to actually implement changes.

**Endexa position**: Technical SEO+GEO audit with code analysis, schema validation, and Jira export speaks directly to dev teams and technical agencies.

### Gap 6: Scoring is superficial across the market
Most tools produce a single "visibility score" based on mention frequency. None have a multi-criteria scoring framework that evaluates crawlability, content structure, structured data, and authority independently.

**Endexa position**: 53-criteria scoring across 4 weighted categories with weakest-link aggregation. Research-backed weights. This is the deepest scoring system in the market.

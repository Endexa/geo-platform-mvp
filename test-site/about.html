About Acme Analytics

Acme Analytics was founded in 2022 by Jane Smith and Mark Chen with a simple mission: make AI-powered analytics accessible to every business, not just Fortune 500 companies.

Our Mission

We believe every team deserves data-driven decision making. Our platform eliminates the need for dedicated data engineers by using AI to automatically connect, clean, and analyze business data.

The Team

Mark Chen, CTO & Co-founder -- Previously led the analytics team at Google Cloud. Expert in distributed systems and real-time data processing.
